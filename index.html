Rubén Sánchez-Janssen

Director, Isaac Newton Group of Telescopes

About

As Director of the Isaac Newton Group of Telescopes (ING), I oversee operations of the 4.2 m William Herschel Telescope and the 2.5 m Isaac Newton Telescope on La Palma. I lead the observatory's staff and day-to-day operations, work to maximise scientific productivity and technical reliability, and collaborate closely with funding agencies and partner communities to guide the observatory's long-term strategic and scientific direction.
